The Heartline mark.

Iteration on the current chart-hill logo — more coaster-forward, with the community's heart in it. A heartline is a real coaster element: the track your body traces through a loop. Same brand palette (ink, coral, accent), new story. Everything below is generated from one traced SVG; variants are just fills.
