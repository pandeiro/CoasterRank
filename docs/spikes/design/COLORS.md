Primary: Deep indigo/ink (#0F0E17 or #1A1A2E) – not just dark, but moody. Think night sky at a seaside amusement pier.

Secondary: Warm cream/paper (#F5F0E8 or #FEFCF3) – vintage ticket stock feel, not cold white.

Accent: "Neon Bulb" – a single, punchy accent color. I'd propose:

    Option A: Coral/pink-orange (#FF6B6B or #E85D75) – evokes neon lights, carnival signage, energy.

    Option B: Electric teal/cyan (#00D4FF or #48CAE4) – evokes track rails, night rides, tech/data feel.

    Option C: Mustard/amber (#E6B91E or #FFC857) – vintage boardwalk, but earthy not "gold medal."

Tertiary: A muted slate/steel for secondary info (#4A4A5A).
